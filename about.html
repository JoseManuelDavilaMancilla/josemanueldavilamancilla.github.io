the longer story

i'm a mexican computer scientist doing my master's at Tsinghua University, in the MOE Key Lab of Pervasive Computing, advised by Prof. Shi Yuanchun and Prof. Wang Yuntao. i work on physiobehavioral and ubiquitous computing — teaching devices to notice the things about us that we don't notice ourselves.

before that, i studied Data Science & Mathematics at ITESM, where i graduated magna cum laude, top 1% of my class. in between i spent a semester on exchange at Fudan University — the one that rerouted everything — and later joined the Global Talent Program in Shanghai, one of 40 selected out of 450.

i came to china to learn how technology gets built at a scale mexico doesn't have yet. long-term, i want to go back home and work on US–China–LatAm collaboration — the kind where latin america is a builder, not just a market.
